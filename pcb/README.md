# Desk companion board

A carrier for an ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin) that plugs into a LoctekMotion / Flexispot control box over RJ45. It runs off the desk's 5 V, shifts the box's 5 V signal down for the ESP32, and uses the jack's two LEDs for Bluetooth (`BT`) and the desk link (`LINK`). The firmware is in [`../proxy`](../proxy).

## Wiring

| RJ45 pin | Control box | On the board | ESP32 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 5 V | PTC fuse, SS14 diode | VIN |
| 7 | GND | ground pour | GND |
| 6 | box TX, 5 V | 10k / 15k divider, BAT54S clamp | GPIO16 (RX2) |
| 5 | box RX | 220 Ω | GPIO17 (TX2) |
| 4 | wake ("PIN 20") | 220 Ω | GPIO23 |
| 12 / 11 | LED | 220 Ω | GPIO32, `BT` |
| 10 / 9 | LED | 220 Ω | GPIO33, `LINK` |

Pins 4 to 8 were measured on my own control box. Measure yours before plugging it in.

For this board, set `CONFIG_LOCTEK_TX_GPIO=17` and `CONFIG_LOCTEK_RX_GPIO=16` in the companion firmware. Its defaults are for the breadboard it was first built on.

## Ordering

Upload `fab/tab5-desk-ctrl-gerbers.zip`, `-bom.csv` and `-cpl.csv` to JLCPCB with SMT assembly on the top side. The jack and the two headers are hand-soldered, see `fab/tab5-desk-ctrl-hand-solder.csv`. Print `fab/tab5-desk-ctrl-check-1to1.pdf` at 100 % and hold the jack and the DevKit against it before you order.

## Files

The KiCad files are generated by the Python scripts here, so change the scripts rather than the board. `make_fab.sh` builds everything in `fab/`. [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) has my notes from designing it: the rebuild order, the decisions and the traps.
